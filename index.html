ABOUT US

LifeLab

Vision, Mission, Values, Capacity and Strengths

Vision

Empower and engage people and communities in Lebanon to improve their quality of life through greater access to health, education and meaningful opportunities for work, while promoting dignity, inclusion and sustainable development.

Mission

LifeLab works with people and communities, with particular attention to women and youth, through initiatives in health, education and work. Its approach includes livelihoods, agriculture, skills development and empowerment, with the aim of reducing exclusion, expanding opportunities and supporting more equitable and resilient communities.

Values

LifeLab is committed to sustainable and inclusive development while respecting human dignity and the rights of the people and communities with whom it works. Its approach is guided by the following values:

Mutual Respect

Treating people with dignity, courtesy and consideration regardless of differences in background, beliefs, gender, age or experience. Mutual respect means listening to people, valuing their perspectives and encouraging dialogue and peaceful collaboration.

Responsibility and Responsiveness

Responding to the needs and concerns of individuals and communities while recognizing the impact of our actions. LifeLab seeks to remain attentive to changing needs and to respond through practical, responsible and community-centered action.

Transparency and Rule of Law

Maintaining accountability, clear communication and responsible practices throughout our work. LifeLab values transparency in its relationships with communities and stakeholders and respects the laws, procedures and principles that protect individual and community rights.

Equality and Equity

Treating all individuals with respect and dignity while promoting fair access to opportunities and services. LifeLab recognizes that people may face different social and economic barriers and that additional support may be necessary to ensure meaningful participation and access.

Independence

Maintaining an objective and non-discriminatory approach to our work. LifeLab's activities and decisions are guided by community needs, professional responsibility and the objective of creating sustainable social and economic impact.

Our Commitment

Human dignity and inclusion are central to our work.

LifeLab's work is grounded in respect for human dignity, equality, inclusion and protection. These principles guide our approach across health, education, work, livelihoods, agriculture and empowerment initiatives.

We seek to create environments in which people are treated with respect, protected from discrimination and able to participate meaningfully in opportunities and decisions that affect their lives.

Particular attention is given to women, youth and people facing social or economic barriers, with the aim of strengthening their access to knowledge, services, skills and opportunities that support greater independence and well-being.
