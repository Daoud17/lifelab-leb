OUR STRATEGY

LifeLab's Strategy

LifeLab's strategy is centered on improving quality of life and expanding opportunities for people and communities in Lebanon, with particular attention to youth, women, vulnerable families and individuals facing social or economic barriers.

The strategy connects health, education and work with livelihoods, empowerment, social cohesion, responsible governance and humanitarian action. Rather than addressing these challenges separately, LifeLab adopts an integrated approach that recognizes the relationship between economic security, individual well-being, participation and sustainable community development.

This approach seeks to strengthen people's capacity to improve their circumstances while making effective use of available skills, resources and opportunities. It also recognizes the importance of cooperation with communities, local actors and civil society in developing responses that are practical, inclusive and sustainable.

Improving Living Conditions and Developing Economic Capabilities

Improving living conditions requires more than short-term assistance. Individuals and families need opportunities to develop their capabilities, secure sustainable sources of income and participate productively in the economic life of their communities.

LifeLab therefore connects economic development with education, skills, employment, agriculture and entrepreneurship. Strengthening people's ability to work and generate income can improve household resilience while contributing to stronger local economies.

This approach focuses on:

  • Strengthening the capacities of youth and supporting their preparation for productive and sustainable employment.
  • Developing technical, professional and managerial skills in productive sectors, including agriculture and related livelihood activities.
  • Supporting skills development and employment pathways that respond to changing economic and labor-market needs.
  • Encouraging livelihood opportunities connected to sustainable practices, including responsible use of water, environmental resources and alternative approaches to energy.
  • Supporting entrepreneurship, startups, social enterprises and the development of micro, small and medium-sized enterprises.
  • Connecting education and vocational development with practical opportunities for income generation and long-term economic participation.

Youth and Women Empowerment and Support

Youth and women are important contributors to social and economic development and can play a significant role in shaping the future of their communities. LifeLab places their participation at the center of its approach to education, work, livelihoods and community development.

Empowerment requires both access to opportunity and the ability to participate meaningfully. LifeLab therefore seeks to strengthen technical and life skills while creating connections between individuals, communities and economic opportunities.

LifeLab's approach includes:

  • Developing technical and professional skills that can improve access to employment and livelihood opportunities.
  • Strengthening the participation of youth and women in their communities and encouraging meaningful engagement in local development.
  • Creating connections between young people and women so that knowledge, opportunities, experiences and resources can be shared.
  • Developing life skills, communication, problem-solving and leadership capacities.
  • Supporting pathways toward entrepreneurship, employment and greater economic independence.
  • Encouraging women and youth to contribute actively to decisions and initiatives that affect their lives and communities.

Building and Strengthening Civil Peace

Civil peace and social cohesion are essential conditions for sustainable development. Economic pressure, inequality, exclusion and competition over limited resources can increase tension within communities and weaken relationships between different social groups.

LifeLab seeks to support environments in which people can address disagreements constructively, participate in community life and cooperate around shared needs. Education, dialogue and community participation are important elements of this approach.

Areas of action include:

  • Strengthening conflict-resolution, communication and mediation capacities within communities.
  • Improving understanding of the causes of conflict and promoting constructive approaches to preventing and resolving disputes.
  • Engaging community actors and local decision-makers in dialogue, training and cooperative initiatives.
  • Strengthening local organizations and encouraging cooperation between groups working within the same communities.
  • Developing the capacities of people working directly with communities and those responding to situations of tension or conflict.
  • Supporting life-skills and peace-building activities for young people in schools, educational settings and communities.
  • Using arts, culture and community activities as accessible ways to encourage dialogue, participation and peaceful interaction.
  • Encouraging responsible and constructive use of digital platforms and social media.
  • Supporting local mechanisms and community networks that can identify tensions, discuss shared concerns and contribute to practical solutions.

Promoting Governance and Community Participation

Effective local development depends on constructive relationships between people, communities, civil society and the institutions that serve them. Participation, transparency and accountability can help communities identify their needs and contribute to better responses and more effective use of available resources.

LifeLab's approach encourages people to understand their role in community life and strengthens the capacity of local actors to listen, communicate and respond to community needs.

This includes:

  • Educating and training individuals and civil society groups to participate constructively in community development.
  • Strengthening the participation of marginalized individuals and groups and supporting their ability to express needs and concerns.
  • Encouraging accountability, transparency and responsible participation in local decision-making.
  • Strengthening the capacity of local institutions and community actors to respond more effectively to people's needs.
  • Encouraging better use of available resources and stronger coordination between communities and the actors working with them.

Activating Cooperation and Humanitarian Activities

Human dignity and fundamental rights remain important across all areas of LifeLab's work. During periods of crisis, communities may require immediate support while also needing access to information, services and opportunities that help them recover and maintain dignified living conditions.

Humanitarian action is therefore connected to LifeLab's broader work in health, education, protection, livelihoods and empowerment. Cooperation with community actors can strengthen the ability to identify urgent needs and respond in a coordinated and responsible way.

LifeLab's humanitarian and cooperation approach includes:

  • Supporting rapid-response interventions when communities face urgent crisis-related needs.
  • Conducting awareness activities that strengthen knowledge of rights, available support and community protection.
  • Supporting women through awareness, participation and empowerment initiatives.
  • Engaging communities and local actors in promoting dignity, inclusion and respect for human rights.
  • Strengthening cooperation between organizations, communities and other relevant actors when responding to humanitarian and development needs.
  • Supporting vulnerable communities and displaced populations through approaches that respect dignity, inclusion and access to essential services.
